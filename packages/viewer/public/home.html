THE WHOLE INTERFACE

Four blocks. Nothing else to configure.

Relay is a caption encoder, not a settings form. Every control sits in one strip under the live text — and it goes read-only the moment you’re on air, so there is nothing to fumble mid-game.

01SOURCE

Any mic or system audio

Pick a device once. A level meter proves it’s hearing you before you go live — no more silent streams.

MIC · SYSTEM · LOOPBACK
02TRANSCRIBE

Streaming speech-to-text

Interim words land as you talk, then settle into a final line. Deepgram Nova-3 on your key — Relay adds no markup.

NOVA-3 · METERED PER MINUTE
03TRANSLATE

Line by line, cached

Optional. Each finished line is translated on its own so nothing waits for a paragraph; repeats come from cache. Skip the key and Relay runs English-only.

GEMINI FLASH-LITE · BYPASSABLE
04OUTPUT

Phone link, OBS, or both

Hit start and you get a link to paste in chat or add as a browser source. On the same network it runs without touching a relay server.

LINK · BROWSER SOURCE · LAN

WHAT IT COSTS

Relay itself is free. You pay Deepgram and Gemini directly at their rates, and the console keeps a running estimate while you’re on air.

PRIVACY

Keys never leave your machine, and neither does your audio — it goes to Deepgram only. When you share a public link, finished captions pass through this relay and nothing else does. No account, no telemetry.

UPDATES

The installed app checks for new builds in the background and applies them on the next restart, so a session is never interrupted.
